# Biolectrics

**Biolectrics** is our working model of how **stress-driven glutamate upregulation** alters cellular bioelectric states to push networks toward **calcium overload, mitochondrial ROS, and maladaptive remodeling**. We map how receptor density/sensitivity (e.g., **NMDA / AMPA**), calcium handling (VGCCs, ER–mitochondria coupling), and redox signaling interact to reinforce excitotoxic loops—and where interventions can break them.

### What “Biolectrics” means in this model
- **Signal → State:** neuromodulators and receptor expression shift **membrane potential** and **ionic set-points**, not just synaptic probability.  
- **State → Metabolism:** sustained depolarizing load increases **ATP demand** and **mitochondrial ROS**, remodeling lipids/proteins and receptor function.  
- **Feedback:** ROS & lipid changes **bias** channels/receptors (e.g., NMDAR), reinforcing excitatory tone unless interrupted.  
- **Interventions:** target **receptor gain**, **calcium entry**, **mito-ER coupling**, **antioxidant/transcriptional programs**, and **lipid repair**.
